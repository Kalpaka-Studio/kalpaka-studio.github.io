Thuruppu 56 Privacy Policy

Kalpaka Studio · Effective 22 November 2026

Thuruppu 56 is a card game made by Kalpaka Studio. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: playing against the computer sends nothing anywhere. Playing with friends online sends only the name you type and the game itself to our game server, which deletes the table after a day without play. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Playing against the computer

When you play against the computer bots, the game works completely offline and makes no network connections. It saves the following only on your own device so you can carry on where you left off:

  • the match in progress (cards, scores and game log) and your running career score;
  • the player names you choose, and your bot-speed and scoring settings.

You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the game.

Playing with friends online

If you choose Play with friends, the game connects to the Kalpaka Studio game server so everyone at the table can play together. Only then is the following sent to the server:

  • the name you type for the table (it is shown to the other players at that table);
  • a random identifier created by the game on your device, so the table recognises you if you reconnect. It is not linked to your phone, account or identity;
  • the table code and your moves in the game.

The server keeps this only for the life of the table. A table is deleted automatically after 24 hours without play, and when you leave a table your seat and name are removed from it. We do not use this information for anything except running the game, and we do not build profiles or keep player lists.

The game server runs on Cloudflare, which, like any internet service, handles your device's IP address to deliver the connection and may keep short-lived technical logs for security and reliability. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

When you share a table, the game uses your phone's own share sheet (for example WhatsApp); what you send there is between you and that app.

Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or share your information. Your table name is visible only to the other players at your table.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Online play asks only for a name to show at the table; any nickname will do.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the effective date above before that change is released.
