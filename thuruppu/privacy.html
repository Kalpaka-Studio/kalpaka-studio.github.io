Thuruppu 56 Privacy Policy

Kalpaka Studio · Effective 20 November 2026

Thuruppu 56 is a card game made by Kalpaka Studio. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: the game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. Thuruppu 56 has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for your contacts, location, photos, microphone or any other personal data.

Information stored on your device

To let you carry on where you left off, the game saves the following only on your own device:

  • the match in progress (cards, scores and game log);
  • the player names you choose, and your bot-speed setting.

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to Kalpaka Studio or anyone else. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the game.

Internet access

The game works completely offline and does not send or receive data over the internet. The standard Android internet permission is included by the app framework that the game is built with, but the game itself makes no network connections.

Sharing of information

Because we do not collect any information, we do not sell, rent or share it with anyone.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding online play, we will update this page and the effective date above before that change is released.
